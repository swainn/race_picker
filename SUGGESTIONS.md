# Battle Bot Picker - Improvement Suggestions

## Gameplay
1. **Shield/defensive ability** — some bots could spawn with a temporary shield that absorbs a hit or two, adding tactical variety
2. **Health pickups** — spawn occasional health packs on the arena floor that bots path toward when low HP
3. **Knockback** — hammer and fireball hits push the target back, potentially into walls/obstacles for bonus damage
4. **Critical hits** — small chance (e.g. 5%) for a hit to deal 2x damage with a special visual flash

## Visual Polish
5. **Damage numbers** — floating numbers that drift upward on each hit showing the damage dealt
6. **Bot weapon indicator** — show the weapon emoji/icon near each bot so spectators know who has what
7. **Victory animation** — when a bot wins (last one standing), play a celebration effect (confetti, spotlight) before the dialog
8. **HP bar colors** — transition from green to yellow to red as HP drops

## Arena
9. **Arena hazards** — lava pits or spike traps that deal periodic damage to any bot standing on them
10. **Destructible crates** — some obstacles break when hit, opening new paths mid-fight
11. **Shrinking arena** — after the rage timer, the arena walls slowly close in, forcing bots together

## UX
12. **Battle log** — a scrolling text feed showing key events ("Alice hit Bob with Hammer for 38 dmg")
13. **Pre-battle weapon reveal** — briefly show each bot with their assigned weapon before the fight starts
14. **Speed controls** — 1x / 2x / 3x playback speed toggle
